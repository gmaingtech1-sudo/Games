# Coin Empire

An idle money tycoon for phones. Start with one lemonade stand and grow to space mining.

- **Tap the big coin** for cash. Upgrades make each tap worth more, and "Smart Taps" add a slice of your income to every tap.
- **Businesses:** Lemonade Stand, Taco Truck, Coffee Shop, Bakery, Gym, Game Studio, Hotel, Airline, Bank and Space Mining. Tap a business to run it once; buy more of it (×1, ×10, ×100 or Max) to earn more per run. Owning 10, 25, 50, 100… of one doubles its profit each time.
- **Managers** run a business non-stop, even while the game is closed. Come back to a "Welcome back" payout (up to 12 hours).
- **Upgrades:** 56 of them — tap boosts, ×3 per business, and ×2 for everything.
- **Golden coins 🪙** float onto the screen now and then. Catch one for a lump of cash or a 20-second ×7 Gold Rush.
- **Vodka 🍸:** down a shot to get drunk: Tipsy, Buzzed, Drunk, Wasted, then Smashed. Every level makes taps worth more (up to ×6), but the screen sways, you see double, you hiccup and some taps miss. Take a shot while Smashed and you pass out, with a 30-second hangover (taps ×¼). You sober up one level every 20 seconds. The wobble can be turned off in settings.
- **Investors ⭐:** once you've earned enough, sell your empire to start over with investors, each adding +10% profit forever.

Play it in a browser by opening `index.html` (or serving the folder), or install the Android app from [`../coin-empire-android`](../coin-empire-android). Progress saves automatically.
