How to care for your pet

  • Stroke your pet with a finger to pet it. Tap it to make it giggle.
  • Move around the house with the room buttons at the bottom, the arrows, or by swiping sideways on an empty spot.
  • Kitchen: drag a snack onto its mouth, or just tap the snack. The fridge opens the snacks again.
  • Bathroom: scrub your pet with your finger, then tap Rinse or the shower.
  • Bedroom: tap the lamp to turn the lights off so energy refills. It wakes up by itself when rested.
  • Playroom: flick the ball for your pet to chase. The arcade has Star Catch and Bubble Pop, which earn coins for the Shop.
  • Levels: everything you do earns XP. Each new level pays coins and unlocks snacks, hats and wallpapers.
  • Daily goals: three new ones every day. Finish them for coins and XP, and all three for a bonus.
  • Daily rewards: open the game every day for a bigger gift. Day 7 is the best one.
  • Birthdays: your pet has a party every month on the day it hatched, and on your birthday too. Blow out the candles and open the present in the living room.
  • Stickers: collect all 16 in the sticker album (the checklist button).
  • Friends: tap the friends button to host a playdate or join one with a code. Your pets visit, wave, swap snacks and duel at Bubble Pop. Bubble Pop for 2 in the arcade is for two players on one phone.
  • Tap the thought bubble (or a need meter) to go straight to the room that helps.
  • Tap poop to clean it up. A dot on a room button means there's a mess in that room.
  • Needs keep changing while the app is closed. If your pet gets sick, give it medicine.

Your current pet, coins and hats will be gone. Tap again to confirm.
