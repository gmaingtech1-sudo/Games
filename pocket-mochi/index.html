How to care for your pet

  • Stroke your pet with a finger to pet it. Tap it to make it giggle.
  • Feed by dragging a snack onto its mouth, or just tap the snack.
  • Wash by scrubbing it with your finger, then tap Rinse.
  • Tap poop to clean it up before the room gets stinky.
  • Sleep turns the lights off so energy refills. It wakes up by itself when rested.
  • Play Star Catch to earn coins, then spend them in the Shop.
  • Needs keep changing while the app is closed. If your pet gets sick, give it medicine.

Your current pet, coins and hats will be gone. Tap again to confirm.
