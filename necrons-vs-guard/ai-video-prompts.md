# The Silent Harvest: AI video prompts

Shot-by-shot prompts for a photorealistic Necrons vs Imperial Guard short film, written for text-to-video tools (Veo, Sora, Runway, Kling and similar). Most tools make 5–10 second clips, so each shot below is one clip. Cut them together in any editor and add the audio from the last section.

Generate in 16:9, 24 fps. If your tool supports it, upload the first frame of the previous shot as a reference so the characters stay consistent.

## Style block

Paste this at the end of every shot prompt so the look stays the same:

> Live-action cinematic war film, shot on ARRI Alexa 65 with anamorphic lenses, night scene, desaturated palette with sickly green highlights, heavy atmospheric haze, ash and dust in the air, handheld camera with slight shake, shallow depth of field, 35mm film grain, practical lighting, photorealistic, gritty and grounded. No text, no logos, no subtitles.

## Character descriptions

Reuse these exact descriptions in every shot that features them.

- **Necron warrior:** a tall, gaunt skeletal android of scratched, dull silver-grey metal, with a smooth skull-like face, glowing green eyes and a ribbed spine and ribcage. It walks stiffly, upright, and carries a long rifle with a glowing green tube. Green energy leaks from its joints.
- **Imperial Guardsman:** an exhausted soldier in a mud-stained olive-drab greatcoat, flak armour and a rounded steel helmet, carrying a rugged lasgun with a wooden stock. Dirty face, wide eyes.
- **Commissar:** a stern officer in a long black coat with a red sash and a peaked cap with a skull badge, holding a humming power sword with a blue-white blade.
- **Tank:** a squat, heavily armoured battle tank in olive paint with scratched armour, a huge main gun, sponson guns and a stack of sandbags and spare tracks on its hull.
- **Pyramid ship:** a colossal black stone pyramid hovering in the sky, with glowing green lines running down its faces and lightning crawling over its surface.

## Shots

**1. Establishing: the tomb wakes (6 s)**
Slow push-in across a battlefield of craters and barbed wire under a pale moon. In the distance a ruined gothic city burns. On the right, stepped black stone pyramids start to glow with green hieroglyphs, one line at a time.

**2. The trench line (6 s)**
Low tracking shot along a muddy trench behind sandbags. Guardsmen crouch with lasguns, breath misting in the cold, faces lit by the green glow in the distance. One soldier quietly mutters a prayer.

**3. The dead rise (6 s)**
Wide shot of no man's land. Columns of green light burst from the ground and Necron warriors stand up out of them, one by one, in perfect silence. Green mist rolls across the mud.

**4. First contact (8 s)**
Over-the-shoulder shot from behind a Guardsman as the Necron line advances at a steady walk. Red lasgun bolts streak across the frame and spark off silver armour. The Necrons answer with green beams that turn a sandbag wall to glowing dust.

**5. Getting back up (6 s)**
Close-up of a Necron warrior hit in the chest by a lasgun bolt. It falls backwards into the mud. A beat later green light crawls across its body, it twitches, and it stands up again. The Guardsman watching it lowers his gun in horror.

**6. The tank (6 s)**
Low-angle hero shot of the tank crashing through a wall of barbed wire. The turret swings and the main gun fires, with a huge muzzle flash and a shockwave that blows dust and mud outwards. Cut to a distant explosion tossing several Necrons into the air.

**7. The ship arrives (8 s)**
The sky darkens. The camera tilts up as a gigantic black pyramid ship descends through the clouds, lightning crawling over its surface. Guardsmen stare up with green light on their faces. The ship hangs overhead, humming.

**8. One shot (6 s)**
A blinding green beam fires from the apex of the pyramid and hits the tank. The tank glows white-green, melts and detonates in a massive fireball, blasting soldiers off their feet.

**9. The commissar (6 s)**
Slow-motion close-up of the commissar standing among smoke and sparks, coat whipping in the blast wind. He raises the power sword, blade crackling, and shouts the order to charge.

**10. The charge (6 s)**
Wide slow-motion shot of the surviving Guardsmen charging across the cratered field into a wall of green fire and advancing silver figures. Everything cuts to white.

**11. Epilogue (4 s)**
Black screen, then a final shot of an empty battlefield at dawn, a single helmet in the mud, green mist fading. Fade out.

## Audio

Most video tools generate sound with the picture. If yours doesn't, or you want to replace it, use these notes. The original soundtrack in `necrons-vs-guard.mp4` can also be reused for timing.

- **Music:** a low, droning synth pad, then slow war drums building at about 100 bpm from shot 4, then a swelling male choir for shots 9 and 10.
- **Effects:** a thin electric crackle for lasguns, a deep rising hum for gauss beams, a scraping metal sound when Necrons stand up, a thunderous bass thump for the tank, and a low sub-bass drone for the pyramid ship.
- **Silence:** a one-second silence after the white flash in shot 10 makes the epilogue land.

## Tips

- Name the faction details in every prompt; video tools tend to drift into generic sci-fi unless you repeat the descriptions.
- Avoid copyrighted names like "Warhammer" in the prompts, since some tools filter them. The character descriptions above are enough.
- If the Necrons come out too clean or too human, add "no flesh, no skin, purely metallic" to the prompt.
- Generate several takes per shot and pick the best; consistency between shots is the hard part, so reuse reference frames.
