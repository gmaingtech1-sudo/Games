How to play

Move one cell at a time. Pick up every shard to open the exit, then step onto it. Par is the fewest moves the puzzle can be solved in: match it for three stars.

    Moving

    Use the pad, swipe on the board (1D and 2D) or tap a glowing neighbour (3D and up). In 3D and up, drag the view to turn it. The pad turns with it, so up on the pad is always away from you.

    From 4D on, the panels above the view show every 3D slice. W moves you from one slice to the next (ana is +W, kata is −W); in 5D, V moves you down the grid. A dashed ring in a panel shows where a jump would land. Tap a panel to look into that slice without moving.
