HOW TO PLAY

Shooting

Touch the table to point the cue at your finger. Drag to move it.

Fine aim: slide along the ribbed strip for tiny adjustments.

Power: drag right along the power bar, then let go to shoot. Slide back to the start to cancel.

Spin: tap the little white ball. Hit low to screw the cue ball back, high to follow through.

8-Ball

Break, then the first ball you pot makes you solids (1–7) or stripes (9–15). Pot all of yours, then the black 8 to win.

Pot one of yours and you go again. Fouls give the other player the cue ball in hand: they can drag it anywhere.

Fouls: potting the cue ball, missing everything, hitting the other group (or the 8) first, or nothing touching a cushion after the hit.

Pot the 8 early, or foul while potting it, and you lose.

Blitz

90 seconds on the clock. Every pot scores and adds 2 seconds. Pot on shot after shot to build a combo up to x5. The black 8 is worth 3. Potting the white costs 5 seconds. Clear the table for a bonus and a fresh rack.
