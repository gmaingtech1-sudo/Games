# Job Interview Simulator

A slapstick job interview at Synergex Solutions Ltd. Every question comes with a couple of sensible answers and a couple of less sensible ones: shoot the interviewer, punch them, throw your coffee, lob the stapler, flip the desk or smash a chair over their head. You can also tap the interviewer for a cheeky slap any time.

- 12 questions per interview, drawn from a pool of 20.
- Knock out Gary the Regional Manager and Karen from HR turns up. Knock her out and the CEO comes in.
- Violent answers in a row build a **Rampage** combo that hits harder.
- Polite answers raise your hire chance. Violence usually lowers it, but sometimes they're impressed.
- Endings: Hired, Hired... Somehow, We'll Be In Touch, Rejected, Escorted Out and Hostile Takeover (knock out all three).

Open `index.html` in a phone browser to play, or install the [Android app](../job-interview-simulator-android/).
