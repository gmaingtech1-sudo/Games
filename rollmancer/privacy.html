Rollmancer privacy policy

Last updated 16 November 2026

Rollmancer is a single-player dice game. It was built so that it doesn't need to know anything about you.

What we collect

Nothing. Rollmancer has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases. It doesn't ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information.

Your save

Your progress (settings, unlocks, statistics and the run in progress) is stored only on your device: in the app's private storage on Android, or in your browser's local storage on the web. It is never sent anywhere. Uninstalling the app, or clearing the site's data in your browser, deletes it. You can also wipe it yourself under Settings → Reset all progress.

Network access

The Android app doesn't request internet access at all: the game, its art, sounds and fonts are all inside it. The web version is downloaded from the website you open it on, and that site's host may keep ordinary server logs (such as IP addresses) as any website does; once loaded, the game itself sends nothing back.

Sharing

If you tap Share result, the game hands a short line of text (your run summary) to your device's share sheet or clipboard. Where it goes from there is up to you.

Permissions

The Android app asks for one permission, vibrate, so dice can give a little haptic tap. You can switch vibration off in Settings.

Children

Rollmancer collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
