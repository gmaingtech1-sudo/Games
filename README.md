# Games

Small games for phones. Each one lives in its own folder.

| Game | Description |
| --- | --- |
| [Pocket Mochi](pocket-mochi/) | A virtual pet for touchscreen phones with a whole house to explore: feed and water it in the kitchen, bathe it, tuck it into bed (or perk it up with an energy drink instead), play ball, toys or arcade games in the playroom, take it for a walk in the garden, preen in front of the attic mirror and stargaze from the rooftop. Levels, daily and weekly goals, rewards, birthday parties, outfits, a sticker album, playdates with a friend's pet online (bubble pop duels, sleepovers) or two players on one phone, and reminders while you're away — look after it well, or it can get sick and, if truly neglected, pass away. |
| [Pocket Mochi for Android](pocket-mochi-android/) | The same game as an installable Android app. [Download the APK](pocket-mochi-android/dist/pocket-mochi.apk). |
| [Pet Cam](pet-cam/) | An AR pet that lives in your phone's camera. Drop it on your floor, then pet it, toss it treats, play fetch and take photos together. |
| [Pet Cam for Android](pet-cam-android/) | The same game as an installable Android app. [Download the APK](pet-cam-android/dist/pet-cam.apk). |
| [Riftborn](riftborn/) | An AR game on your real streets, mixing Ingress, Pokémon GO and Jurassic World Alive. Fight over Rifts for your faction, catch prehistoric creatures in your camera, collect their DNA and fuse hybrids. |
| [Cosmos 360](cosmos-360/) | A to-scale universe to explore in 360°. Look around by dragging or moving your phone, fly to real planets and moons where they are today, scan them for your logbook, and jump to Alpha Centauri, TRAPPIST-1 and the black hole at the centre of the Milky Way. |
| [Portal Hackers: Nexus](portal-hackers/) | Like Ingress, but different: explore your real streets with a 3D Sci-Fi Compass, hack portals for gear, knock out enemy Uplinks with Pulse Bombs or sabotage hacks, deploy your own, link portals into control fields, and climb 50 levels from Scout to Nexus Master, with accounts, live team chat, a shop, profile customisation, daily and squad missions, team events, weekly team objectives, a Compass upgrade tree and Prestige. |
| [Portal Hackers for Android](portal-hackers-android/) | The same game as an installable Android app. [Download the APK](portal-hackers-android/dist/portal-hackers.apk). |
| [Starforge](starforge/) | A space shooter where you build your own starfighter. Pick its hull, wings, engine, weapon and special, paint it, then blast through waves of enemy ships and bosses to earn credits for better parts. |
